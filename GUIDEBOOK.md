
# Marketing Map — Quick User Guide

Welcome! This guide helps you use the Marketing Map web app. It avoids technical terms and focuses on what you, as an end user, need to know.

**Where to open the app**

- If you received a web address (URL) from your team or admin, open it in your browser.
- If you were given an installed application or desktop link, open it the same way you open other apps.

**Getting started**

- Create an account or sign in using your email or the sign-in option provided by your organization.
- After signing in, you'll land on the main map screen.

**Main screen overview**

- Map: The central area shows locations, regions, or data points.
- Search: Use the search box to find an address, region, or place name.
- Filters / Layers: Turn on or off different data layers (for example: sales, potential, stalls) and apply filters to focus on specific data.
- Details panel: Click a map area or marker to open a side panel with detailed information.

How to interact with the map

- Pan: Click and drag the map to move it.
- Zoom: Use your mouse wheel, trackpad pinch, or the + / - buttons to zoom in and out.
- Click: Select a region or marker to see more details.

Working with filters and layers

- Open the Filters or Layers menu to show only the items you want to see (e.g., specific months, categories, or district types).
- Enable or disable layers to reduce clutter and highlight the information that matters.

Searching and finding locations

- Type a place name, address, or identifier in the search box and press Enter.
- Results will center the map and show a list or highlight the matching areas.

Exporting or sharing data

- Look for an Export or Share button to download data (CSV, image) or copy a shareable link.
- Use the shareable link to let others open the same view and filters.

Account and settings

- Profile: Update your name, email, or preferences in the Account or Profile area.
- Notifications: Toggle whether you receive email updates or alerts from the app.

Accessibility

- The app includes keyboard navigation and high-contrast modes when available. Use the Settings or Accessibility menu to adjust preferences.

Troubleshooting (quick fixes)

- I can’t sign in: Double-check your email and password. If you still can’t sign in, use the “Forgot password” link or contact your admin.
- The map won’t load: Try refreshing the page. If the problem persists, try a different browser or contact support.
- Search returns no results: Try different keywords or check filters are not hiding results.

Getting help

- Contact your project admin or support team (they can provide access, troubleshoot, or reset your account).
- If a feedback or support link is available inside the app, use it to send a message with screenshots and a short description.
